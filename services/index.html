Why AI PoCs Often Fail

Common causes we’ve seen across industries

“A staggering 85% of AI models fail to reach production due to misalignment with real business needs, poor data pipelines, or lack of stakeholder buy-in.”

— Forbes Technology Council, 2024
  • 📉 Lack of real business alignment — teams build models without a clear ROI or KPI.
  • ⚙️ Over-engineered tech — fancy algorithms, but no measurable value or end-user buy-in.
  • 🌉 No bridge between PoC and productization — the prototype never makes it into a production pipeline.
  • 💼 Missing in-house AI talent or process maturity — no one to maintain, monitor, or scale the model.
