Our Mission

Build AI systems that solve real business problems — and deploy them to production where they create measurable value. No PoC graveyards.

Our Vision

A world where production AI is accessible to any enterprise team — not just those with in-house data science departments. AI Solutions bridges that gap.

What We Do

AI Solutions is an applied AI engineering practice. We design, build, and deploy AI systems for enterprise clients across Healthcare, Life Sciences, HR, Manufacturing, and Financial Services.

Applied AI Engineering

We build RAG systems, LLM applications, computer vision pipelines, and ML models — all the way to production deployment and monitoring.

CRISP-ML(Q) Delivery

Every engagement follows CRISP-ML(Q) — the industry-standard AI delivery framework. Defined quality gates. No surprises at handoff.

Research-Backed Practice

In partnership with CITEDI, CETYS, UABC, and ITT-TecNM, we stay at the frontier of AI research — and bring that into applied delivery work.

Our Engineering Team
