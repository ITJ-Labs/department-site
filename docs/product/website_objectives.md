## Purpose

This document defines the business purpose of the AI Solutions Digital Platform. It answers three questions:

1. Who is this website for?
2. What do we want each visitor to do?
3. How do we measure success?

It is the primary reference for all Information Architecture, content, and platform investment decisions. If a feature, page, or piece of content cannot be justified by one of the goals below, it should not be built.

---

## Primary Audience

### Audience 1 — Enterprise Technology Decision-Makers

**Who they are:** CTOs, Chief AI Officers, Chief Digital Officers, and VPs of Engineering at mid-to-large enterprises (1,000–50,000 employees). They hold budget authority for AI initiatives and technology partnerships.

**What brings them here:** They are evaluating AI vendors after an internal mandate to explore or expand AI. They may have heard about AI Solutions through word of mouth, a conference, or a referral from a technology partner.

**What they are looking for:**
- Evidence that AI Solutions can deliver at enterprise scale
- Credibility signals: clients, industries served, methodologies, compliance posture
- Enough technical depth to trust the team is real
- A clear way to start a conversation

**What makes them leave:** Vague marketing language. Claims without evidence. Inability to find services relevant to their industry.

**Primary conversion goal:** Schedule a discovery call.

**Key pages:** Home, About, Services/Capabilities, Industries, Case Studies, Contact.

### Audience 2 — Senior AI Engineers and Data Scientists

**Who they are:** AI team leads, staff-level engineers, and senior data scientists at companies that either have their own AI teams or are evaluating AI partners. Some are potential hires. Some are peers evaluating AI Solutions as a collaborator or vendor.

**What brings them here:** A technical publication, an open-source reference, a conference talk, or a recommendation from the AI community.

**What they are looking for:**
- Technical depth and methodology transparency
- What technology stack and AI frameworks AI Solutions uses
- Evidence of research-level work (publications, experiments, benchmarks)
- Whether this is a team they would want to work with or hire

**What makes them leave:** Lack of technical detail. Generic "AI transformation" language. No evidence of hands-on engineering work.

**Primary conversion goal:** (Recruiting) Apply to join AI Solutions. (Peer / partner) Contact for collaboration or follow on LinkedIn.

**Key pages:** Publications, Services (technical details), About / Team, Blog / Insights.

## Secondary Audience

### Audience 3 — HR and Talent Operations Leaders

**Who they are:** Directors and VPs of HR, Talent Acquisition, and People Operations. They manage hiring processes, workforce analytics, and recruiting technology.

**What brings them here:** Direct search for AI-powered HR tools. A referral to CV Extractor or TalentBot specifically. An inquiry from their IT team.

**What they are looking for:**
- A specific solution that solves their problem (resume parsing, candidate screening, skills assessment)
- Integration requirements (does it connect to our ATS?)
- Compliance posture (GDPR-compliant?)
- Cost to get started (PoC timeline and scope)

**Primary conversion goal:** Contact for a demo of CV Extractor or TalentBot.

**Key pages:** Solutions (CV Extractor, TalentBot), Contact.

### Audience 4 — Life Sciences and Pharma Compliance Professionals

**Who they are:** Directors of Regulatory Affairs, Clinical Operations, or Document Management at pharma and biotech companies.

**What brings them here:** Referral from IT or procurement. A specific search for AI-powered document management in regulated industries.

**What they are looking for:**
- Whether NeuroDocs addresses their specific compliance context (FDA, EMA, ICH)
- Data governance and audit trail capabilities
- Evidence of pharma or healthcare deployments
- How to request a demo or evaluation

**Primary conversion goal:** Contact for a NeuroDocs demo or evaluation.

**Key pages:** Solutions (NeuroDocs), Industries (Life Sciences), Contact.

### Audience 5 — Technology Partners and Systems Integrators

**Who they are:** ISVs, regional SIs, and cloud provider professional services teams that want to add AI engineering capability to their client delivery.

**What brings them here:** Looking for an AI engineering partner that can augment their team. Evaluating AI Solutions as a subcontractor or co-delivery partner.

**What they are looking for:**
- Delivery capability and capacity
- Accelerator portfolio (can we use NeuroDocs/CV Extractor in our client engagements?)
- Technology stack compatibility with their existing client environments
- Partnership model

**Primary conversion goal:** Contact AI Solutions to discuss a partnership arrangement.

**Key pages:** About, Capabilities, Contact.

## Business Outcomes

### Outcome 1 — Qualified Lead Generation

**Definition:** A website visitor contacts AI Solutions expressing interest in a paid engagement or discovery conversation.

**Why it matters:** This is the primary revenue driver from the website. A single enterprise AI engagement is worth six to seven figures. Even one additional annual contact attributable to the website materially justifies the platform investment.

**Current state:** No structured lead capture. Contact form exists but is not connected to CRM or tracked.

**Target state:** Every contact form submission captured, routed, and logged. Source attribution in place. Monthly reporting on lead count and quality.

**Dependencies:** SF-SLICE-094 (lead capture infrastructure), CRM selection (business decision).

### Outcome 2 — Credibility and Trust Acceleration

**Definition:** The website reduces the due-diligence burden on enterprise buyers. When AI Solutions leadership shares the site URL, the buyer arrives at a site that independently validates claims and accelerates trust.

**Why it matters:** Enterprise AI decisions involve legal, compliance, IT, and procurement. Each stakeholder needs a place to verify claims independently. A credible platform reduces the number of calls, emails, and documents required during an evaluation.

**Target state:** Case studies, capability pages, and publications pages that directly answer the due diligence questions of each buyer persona.

**Dependencies:** SF-SLICE-088 (capability pages), SF-SLICE-090 (case studies), SF-SLICE-091 (publications).

### Outcome 3 — Recruiting Pipeline Support

**Definition:** The website attracts inbound applications from qualified AI engineers and researchers.

**Why it matters:** AI talent is the primary constraint on growth. A site that communicates technical depth, research culture, and interesting work reduces recruiting cost and improves application quality.

**Target state:** Monthly inbound applications from qualified candidates who mention the website. Technical publications page builds the research credential. About/Team page communicates who the people are and what they do.

**Dependencies:** SF-SLICE-091 (publications), SF-SLICE-088 (About page expansion).

### Outcome 4 — Thought Leadership Authority

**Definition:** AI Solutions is recognized as a credible voice in applied AI engineering. Publications, case studies, and technical writing are cited by practitioners and referenced in industry conversations.

**Why it matters:** Authority compounds. A single highly-cited publication or referenced case study can drive years of inbound. Thought leadership also attracts recruiting and partnership opportunities that don't flow through paid channels.

**Target state:** One new publication per quarter. One engineering blog post per month. Regular conference presence. Google Scholar and LinkedIn citation growth.

**Dependencies:** SF-SLICE-091 (publications infrastructure), team investment in research writing.

### Outcome 5 — Platform Self-Demonstration

**Definition:** The AI Solutions Digital Platform itself demonstrates AI Solutions' engineering capability. The fact that the platform is maintained autonomously by the AI Delivery OS is a visible, real proof point.

**Why it matters:** Clients hire AI Solutions to build AI that works. The best evidence is a platform that AI Solutions built and operates continuously with minimal manual intervention. This is a unique differentiator no marketing copy can replicate.

**Target state:** AI Delivery OS missions publish new content, update capability pages, and deploy changes without manual engineering intervention. Platform uptime, CI/CD reliability, and autonomous operation are measurable and citable.

**Dependencies:** SF-SLICE-092 (AI Delivery OS content automation).

## Conversion Goals

Each audience has a primary conversion goal — the action the website should motivate them to take.

| Audience | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA | Form / Contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise Decision-Maker | Schedule a Discovery Call | Download a White Paper | Contact form → CRM |
| Senior AI Engineer (recruiting) | View Open Roles | Follow on LinkedIn | Careers application |
| Senior AI Engineer (peer) | Read Publications | Follow on LinkedIn | Contact form |
| HR / Talent Operations | Request a TalentBot / CV Extractor Demo | Contact for details | Contact form → CRM |
| Life Sciences / Pharma | Request a NeuroDocs Demo | Contact for evaluation | Contact form → CRM |
| Technology Partner | Contact for Partnership | View Capabilities | Contact form → CRM |

### CTA Placement Principles

1. **Every page has one primary CTA.** Not a menu of options — a single clear next step.
2. **Solutions and capability pages always end with a demo request.** The buyer who reads to the end is warm.
3. **Publications and blog posts end with a contact teaser.** "Interested in how we applied this to [industry]? Talk to us."
4. **The homepage CTA is discovery-oriented.** "Talk to our AI team" or "Schedule a call" — not "Buy now."
5. **CTAs are never green.** Green (`--color-success`) is reserved for semantic confirmation states. All primary CTAs use brand blue (`--color-primary`).

## Information Priorities

When making content triage decisions, use this priority order:

### Priority 1 — Evidence of Production AI

Case studies, testimonials, production deployment metrics, uptime claims with evidence, and documented client outcomes. This is the most valuable content on the site. Everything else exists to support or contextualize this evidence.

### Priority 2 — Methodology Transparency

How AI Solutions builds AI. CRISP-ML(Q) methodology. Engineering process. Technology choices and why. Quality gates. This content builds trust with technical evaluators and differentiates from "we use AI" competitors.

### Priority 3 — Capability Depth

What AI Solutions can actually build. Not buzzwords — specific capabilities with technology depth, use cases, and deployment context. This content is required before any discovery call.

### Priority 4 — Team Credibility

Who the people are. Their credentials, publications, and the work they have done. Enterprise buyers buy people as much as they buy capabilities.

### Priority 5 — Thought Leadership

Publications, blog posts, conference content. Long-term compounding asset. Important but secondary to the four priorities above — a publication with no accompanying evidence of delivery is marketing, not differentiation.

### Priority 6 — Company and Culture

Story, values, mission, and origin. Supports recruiting and brand affinity. Not a primary driver of enterprise revenue.

## Executive Priorities

The following priorities are set by AI Solutions executive leadership and must not be overridden by technical or design decisions without executive approval.

### EP-01 — Production Credibility is Non-Negotiable

Every page that makes a claim about AI delivery must be backed by evidence. Marketing language without evidence is explicitly prohibited. If we cannot cite a case study, a metric, or a methodology, we do not publish the claim.

### EP-02 — AI Delivery OS is an Accelerator, Not a Product

The AI Delivery OS (SliceForge) is how we work. It is not what we sell. Do not position it as a product offering. Do reference it as evidence of our delivery capability and engineering culture.

### EP-03 — Compliance is a Feature, Not an Afterthought

GDPR and HIPAA compliance must be visible on every solution page and every industry page that touches regulated data. This is not a footer disclaimer — it is a capability claim.

### EP-04 — Green is Semantic-Only

The color green (`--color-success`, `#2e7d32`) is reserved for semantic confirmation states in the UI. It is NOT used as a brand color, a CTA color, or a decorative accent anywhere on the site. This decision (D-02) was established in SF-SLICE-087D.1 and is not subject to revision without executive review.

### EP-05 — The Platform Must Always Deploy

CI/CD reliability is a business requirement, not a technical nicety. If the platform is broken or offline, it is visible evidence that AI Solutions cannot maintain its own AI systems. Platform reliability is an executive KPI.

### EP-06 — Content Quality Over Quantity

Three excellent case studies outperform twenty thin capability descriptions. One rigorous technical paper outperforms a hundred generic blog posts. Quality gates apply to all content.
