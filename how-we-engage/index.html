Our Methodology

CRISP-ML(Q) — The AI Delivery Standard We Use

CRISP-ML(Q) (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Machine Learning with Quality Assurance) is the industry-recognized framework for structured AI/ML delivery. It defines six phases that take a project from business understanding through production deployment and monitoring.

We use CRISP-ML(Q) as the backbone of our delivery process because it provides:

  • Explicit quality gates before moving between phases
  • Defined decision points where scope or feasibility is reassessed
  • A shared language between our engineers and your stakeholders
  • Audit-ready documentation at each milestone
PHASE 1
Business Understanding
Goals, feasibility, success criteria
PHASE 2
Data Understanding
Data audit, quality assessment
PHASE 3
Data Preparation
Pipelines, labeling, feature engineering
PHASE 4
Modeling
Training, evaluation, selection
PHASE 5
Evaluation
Business validation, sign-off
PHASE 6
Deployment & Monitoring
Production release, drift detection

Engagement Options

Project

Fixed-Scope Project

Defined deliverables, timeline, and price. Best for well-scoped problems with clear success criteria and stable requirements. Includes the full CRISP-ML(Q) delivery lifecycle.

Embedded

Embedded Team

AI engineers embedded with your product or data team. Best for organizations building internal AI capability or managing a portfolio of AI initiatives across product lines.

Advisory

AI Strategy Advisory

Technical advisory for executives and product leaders. AI opportunity identification, build vs. buy assessment, data readiness audit, and AI program governance design. No implementation — pure strategy and architecture guidance.
