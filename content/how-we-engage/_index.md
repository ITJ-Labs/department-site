    <h2 class="ds-heading-2">CRISP-ML(Q) — The AI Delivery Standard We Use</h2>
    <div class="ds-grid-2" style="margin-top:1.5rem;">
      <div>
        <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
          CRISP-ML(Q) (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Machine Learning with Quality Assurance)
          is the industry-recognized framework for structured AI/ML delivery. It defines six phases
          that take a project from business understanding through production deployment and monitoring.
        </p>
        <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:1rem;">
          We use CRISP-ML(Q) as the backbone of our delivery process because it provides:
        </p>
        <ul style="padding-left:1.2rem; line-height:2; color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
          <li>Explicit quality gates before moving between phases</li>
          <li>Defined decision points where scope or feasibility is reassessed</li>
          <li>A shared language between our engineers and your stakeholders</li>
          <li>Audit-ready documentation at each milestone</li>
        </ul>
      </div>

    <h2 class="ds-heading-2" style="text-align:center; margin-bottom:3rem;">Our Four-Phase Delivery Model</h2>

    <div style="display:grid; gap:2rem;">

      <div class="ds-card" style="padding:2rem; display:grid; grid-template-columns:auto 1fr; gap:2rem; align-items:start;">
        <div style="font-size:2.5rem; font-weight:800; color:var(--color-primary); line-height:1;">01</div>
        <div>
          <h3 class="ds-heading-3">Discovery Call</h3>
          <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-bottom:1rem;">
            A focused 60-minute session to assess your AI opportunity and determine fit. We cover:
            the business problem you're solving, your data landscape, integration requirements,
            and whether AI is the right tool for this use case.
          </p>
          <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
            Output: a written feasibility assessment with a recommended path forward —
            or an honest assessment that AI is not the right tool for this problem.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ds-card" style="padding:2rem; display:grid; grid-template-columns:auto 1fr; gap:2rem; align-items:start;">
        <div style="font-size:2.5rem; font-weight:800; color:var(--color-primary); line-height:1;">02</div>
        <div>
          <h3 class="ds-heading-3">Scoped Pilot</h3>
          <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-bottom:1rem;">
            A time-boxed, production-grade prototype that proves feasibility on your actual data —
            not synthetic benchmarks. The pilot is designed to answer the one question that matters:
            can this work in your environment?
          </p>
          <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
            We set explicit success criteria at the start and present results at the end. If the
            pilot doesn't pass the success criteria, we tell you — and explain why.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ds-card" style="padding:2rem; display:grid; grid-template-columns:auto 1fr; gap:2rem; align-items:start;">
        <div style="font-size:2.5rem; font-weight:800; color:var(--color-primary); line-height:1;">03</div>
        <div>
          <h3 class="ds-heading-3">Production Build</h3>
          <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-bottom:1rem;">
            Full system development with MLOps infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and integration
            into your existing tech stack. We follow CRISP-ML(Q) quality gates throughout —
            each phase produces documentation that supports both internal review and external audit.
          </p>
          <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
            Output: a production-ready AI system, deployed in your infrastructure or ours,
            with monitoring, alerting, and a model maintenance plan.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="ds-card" style="padding:2rem; display:grid; grid-template-columns:auto 1fr; gap:2rem; align-items:start;">
        <div style="font-size:2.5rem; font-weight:800; color:var(--color-primary); line-height:1;">04</div>
        <div>
          <h3 class="ds-heading-3">Ongoing Support & Optimization</h3>
          <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-bottom:1rem;">
            Production AI degrades over time. Model drift, data distribution shifts, and changing
            business requirements mean that AI systems need ongoing monitoring and retraining.
            We offer retainer-based support for model monitoring, retraining, and iterative improvement.
          </p>
          <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
            Output: monthly model health reports, quarterly retraining reviews, and on-call
            response for production incidents.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
