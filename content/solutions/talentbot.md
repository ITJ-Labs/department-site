    <div>
      <h2 class="ds-heading-3">The Problem It Solves</h2>
      <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
        Recruiting teams spend significant time on repetitive tasks: screening candidates against
        role requirements, preparing interview questions, scoring responses, and coordinating
        next steps. These tasks are well-defined enough to automate — but most HR tools don't.
      </p>
      <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:1rem;">
        TalentBot handles the structured, high-volume parts of the recruitment workflow through
        conversational AI — freeing recruiters to focus on relationship-building and final evaluation.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div>
      <h2 class="ds-heading-3">Key Applications</h2>
      <ul style="padding-left:1.2rem; line-height:2; color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
        <li>Automated initial candidate screening via chat</li>
        <li>Role-specific interview question generation</li>
        <li>Pre-hiring assessment and structured scoring</li>
        <li>Candidate FAQ and process communication</li>
        <li>Recruiter productivity dashboards and reporting</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

  <h2 class="ds-heading-3 ds-mb-6">How It Works</h2>
  <div class="ds-grid-3 ds-mb-12">
    <div class="ds-card">
      <strong>Role Configuration</strong>
      <p style="font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:0.5rem;">
        Recruiters define role requirements, screening criteria, and assessment parameters.
        TalentBot learns the evaluation framework for each open position.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="ds-card">
      <strong>Conversational Screening</strong>
      <p style="font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:0.5rem;">
        Candidates interact with TalentBot to answer initial screening questions. The system
        assesses responses against role criteria using domain-fine-tuned LLM evaluation.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="ds-card">
      <strong>Structured Output</strong>
      <p style="font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:0.5rem;">
        Each screened candidate gets a structured summary: fit score, key signals, recommended
        next steps. Recruiters review summaries rather than raw conversation transcripts.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
