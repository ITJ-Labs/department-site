      <h2 class="ds-heading-3">The Problem It Solves</h2>
      <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
        Life Sciences and Pharma teams manage thousands of regulatory documents — clinical trial reports,
        SOPs, regulatory filings, audit records. Finding answers across this corpus manually takes hours
        and introduces risk of oversight.
      </p>
      <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:1rem;">
        NeuroDocs deploys a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system on your document corpus,
        making it queryable in natural language. Analysts ask a question; the system finds the
        relevant sections across all documents and returns a precise, traceable answer.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div>
      <h2 class="ds-heading-3">Key Applications</h2>
      <ul style="padding-left:1.2rem; line-height:2; color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
        <li>Regulatory filing and compliance Q&A</li>
        <li>Clinical trial document retrieval</li>
        <li>SOP search and version comparison</li>
        <li>Audit preparation — trace answers to source documents</li>
        <li>Cross-document consistency checks</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

  <h2 class="ds-heading-3 ds-mb-6">How It Works</h2>
  <div class="ds-grid-3 ds-mb-12">
    <div class="ds-card">
      <strong>Document Ingestion</strong>
      <p style="font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:0.5rem;">
        PDF, DOCX, and HTML documents are ingested, chunked, and stored in a vector database optimized
        for semantic retrieval.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="ds-card">
      <strong>Retrieval-Augmented Generation</strong>
      <p style="font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:0.5rem;">
        Natural language queries retrieve the most relevant document sections. A fine-tuned LLM
        synthesizes the answer from retrieved context — not from model memory.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="ds-card">
      <strong>Traceable Answers</strong>
      <p style="font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:0.5rem;">
        Every answer includes source citations — document name, section, page. Analysts can verify
        any answer against the original document.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
