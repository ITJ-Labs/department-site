      <h2 class="ds-heading-3">The Problem It Solves</h2>
      <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
        Enterprise recruiting teams receive hundreds of CVs per open role. Manual review is slow,
        inconsistent, and expensive. Most ATS platforms parse resumes poorly — missing skills,
        misclassifying experience levels, and requiring manual correction.
      </p>
      <p style="color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:1rem;">
        CV Extractor processes PDF, DOCX, and RTF resumes through an NLP pipeline that extracts
        structured data — skills, experience, education, contact info — and outputs clean JSON and
        CSV ready for downstream analytics or ATS ingestion.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div>
      <h2 class="ds-heading-3">Key Applications</h2>
      <ul style="padding-left:1.2rem; line-height:2; color:var(--color-text-secondary);">
        <li>Bulk resume processing for high-volume recruiting</li>
        <li>ATS pre-population with structured candidate data</li>
        <li>Candidate ranking by skills and experience match</li>
        <li>Talent pipeline analytics and sourcing intelligence</li>
        <li>Resume screening automation for staffing agencies</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

  <h2 class="ds-heading-3 ds-mb-6">How It Works</h2>
  <div class="ds-grid-3 ds-mb-12">
    <div class="ds-card">
      <strong>Ingestion Pipeline</strong>
      <p style="font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:0.5rem;">
        Resumes arrive via file upload, email, or event-driven pipeline (Pub/Sub).
        PDF, DOCX, and RTF formats are parsed with OCR for scanned documents.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="ds-card">
      <strong>NLP Extraction</strong>
      <p style="font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:0.5rem;">
        Named entity recognition and structured information extraction identifies
        skills, titles, employers, dates, education, and contact information with
        high precision across diverse resume formats.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="ds-card">
      <strong>Structured Output</strong>
      <p style="font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--color-text-secondary); margin-top:0.5rem;">
        Output delivered as JSON (per candidate) and CSV (batch) — formatted to
        match your ATS schema or analytics platform. Integration with downstream
        systems via REST API or file export.
      </p>
    </div>
