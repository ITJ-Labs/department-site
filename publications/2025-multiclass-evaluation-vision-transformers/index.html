Multiclass Evaluation of Vision Transformers for Industrial Welding Defect Detection

Feb 28, 2025 • Antonio Contreras Ortiz, Ricardo Rioda Santiago, Daniel E. Hernandez, Miguel Lopez-Montiel

This study evaluates Vision Transformers (ViTs) on the Aluminum 5083 TIG dataset for both binary defect detection and multiclass categorization, achieving 98–99% accuracy—far surpassing prior CNN-based models.

Journal: Mathematical and Computational Applications, 30(2), 24
